1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

TITLE:          Sven Killer Demo
ENGINE:         Godot 4.x
GENRE:          Third-person walking/interaction game
INSPIRATION:    God of War (2018) camera, Gaspar Noé's "I Stand Alone"
DURATION:       ~10-15 minutes total
PLATFORMS:      Windows (primary), Mac/Linux (secondary)


STORY PREMISE:
─────────────
The demo opens with Sven — a man in his late 20s/early 30s — recounting events 
from custody in the far future. Through voiceover and VHS-style footage, we 
understand something significant happened. The player then controls Sven on 
the day in question: waking in his sparse Swedish townhouse, receiving a 
voicemail from his mother, and walking through rain-soaked streets to meet 
a girl at a bus station.

Two Noé-style black screen interruptions punctuate the experience with 
Swedish text and metallic sound effects.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

DISPLAY
───────
• Resolution:       1920 × 1080 (16:9)
• Frame Rate:       60 FPS target
• Aspect Ratio:     Fixed (black bars on ultrawide)

CAMERA
──────
• Style:            Over-the-shoulder, third-person
• Offset from neck: +0.20m X, +0.12m Y, −0.65m Z
• FOV:              ~70° (adjustable)
• Inertia:          Slight lag for cinematic feel
• Collision:        Pushes in when obstructed, no clipping

CONTROLS
────────
• Movement:         WASD / Left Stick
• Camera:           Mouse / Right Stick
• Interact:         E / A Button
• Stand Up:         E (from sitting)
• Light Switch:     E (when near switch)

PLAYER CHARACTER
────────────────
• Walking Speed:    1.2 m/s (casual stroll, non-adjustable)
• No running, jumping, or crouching
• Can sit on designated furniture
• Collidable with pedestrians (they stop if blocked)

SUBTITLES
─────────
• Languages:        Swedish, English, Spanish, Chinese, French, German, Turkish
• Style:            Bottom-center, readable font
• Timing:           Synced to audio

3. GAME FLOW (Complete Sequence)
================================================================================

PHASE A: OPENING (Pre-Gameplay)
───────────────────────────────
A1. Black screen
A2. Voiceover begins (Sven in custody, far future, reflecting)
A3. VHS videos play in sequence (4:3 ratio, CRT filter, centered)
    • Video timing and content provided by you
A4. Videos end → Mission prompt fades in (right side of screen)
    • Swedish text + metallic whoosh sound
A5. Fade to gameplay


PHASE B: HOUSE INTERIOR (Upper Floor)
─────────────────────────────────────
B1. Sven sits on edge of bed, bedroom light ON
B2. All other lights OFF
B3. Player presses E → Sven stands
B4. 15-second exploration timer starts (hidden from player)
B5. Player can:
    • Explore bedroom (wardrobe, nightstand, window)
    • Enter bathroom (toilet, sink, bathtub — non-interactable)
    • Toggle light switches
    • Just proceed downstairs immediately

PHASE C: HOUSE INTERIOR (Ground Floor)
──────────────────────────────────────
C1. Staircase descent (camera follows smoothly)
C2. Ground floor is DARK (streetlight through blinds provides ambient)
C3. Player can:
    • Find and toggle light switches (living room, kitchen)
    • Listen to voicemail on wall phone (mother's message)
    • Read newspaper on coffee table
    • Sit on couch or dining chair
    • Explore freely
C4. Front door is the exit point

TRIGGER: Street environment begins loading when player descends stairs

PHASE D: HOUSE EXIT
───────────────────
D1. Player approaches front door
D2. Presses E to interact
D3. Exit cutscene plays:
    • Sven opens door
    • Steps outside
    • Door closes behind him
    • Sound: Door lock clicking
D4. Door is now LOCKED (can't re-enter)
D5. Player now on street


PHASE E: STREET WALK
────────────────────
E1. Sven on sidewalk outside his house
E2. Destination dot visible (white, toward bus station, through buildings)
E3. Light rain falling (visual + audio)
E4. 5-7 ambient pedestrians visible at any time
    • Walking paths, spawn/despawn off-screen
    • Collidable — stop if Sven blocks them
E5. Cars pass every 6-7 seconds
E6. Death mechanic: Stepping into traffic → black screen → respawn on pavement
E7. Player walks ~72 meters to bus station (~60 seconds)

FIRST NOÉ PROMPT (timing TBD):
• Black screen interruption
• Swedish text + metallic sound
• Duration: ~3-5 seconds
• Returns to gameplay

PHASE F: BUS STATION ARRIVAL
────────────────────────────
F1. Bus shelter visible with the girl
F2. Girl has idle animation (provided by you)
F3. Destination dot disappears when close enough
F4. Player can approach the girl
