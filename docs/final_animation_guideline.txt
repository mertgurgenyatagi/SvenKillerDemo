The camera is not rigidly attached to the character’s head but floats on a flexible "boom" that expands and contracts based on collision and movement state.Pivot Point: The camera orbits around a pivot point located approximately 1.6 meters above the ground (roughly the character's neck/head height).Field of View (FOV): The default vertical FOV is tightly constrained, estimated at ~45-55 degrees (roughly 70-85 degrees horizontal) on console/default settings, creating a cinematic but somewhat tunnel-visioned perspective.Lag/Damping: The camera features rotational damping. When the mouse (yaw) is moved quickly, the camera accelerates and decelerates smoothly rather than snapping instantly. This creates a "weighty" feel, with a lag time of approximately 0.1s - 0.2s between mouse input and final frame alignment.II. Movement Physics (Numerical Analysis)The movement logic is acceleration-based, not velocity-based. Pressing a key does not instantly set speed to max; it applies a force vector.Walk Speed: The standard walking velocity is approximately 1.5 meters per second (m/s) or 5.4 km/h.Acceleration: Upon pressing W, the character takes approximately 0.4 - 0.6 seconds to reach full walking speed. This ramp-up prevents "sliding" animations.Deceleration (Friction): Upon releasing W, the character does not stop instantly. There is a deceleration phase lasting roughly 0.3 - 0.5 seconds, during which the character takes one or two settling steps before returning to a complete 0.0 m/s state.III.
