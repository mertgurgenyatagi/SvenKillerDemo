## Core Systems
**GameManager / Scene transitions**
- Scene changes, subtitle slot arbitration, and hard-cut logic.
- See [scripts/autoload/game_manager.gd](scripts/autoload/game_manager.gd).

**Audio**
- Pooled 2D/3D players, config file, dynamic NoePrompt bus.
- See [scripts/autoload/audio_manager.gd](scripts/autoload/audio_manager.gd) and [resources/audio_config.tres](resources/audio_config.tres).
- Two sources of truth: config file plus call-site overrides (MENU_CLICK, NOE_PROMPT, opening voiceover).

**Subtitles**
- Bottom-slot arbitration via GameManager claim/release.
- Cafe dialogue uses its own CanvasLayer at layer 128.
- See [scripts/scenes/cafe_dialogue.gd](scripts/scenes/cafe_dialogue.gd).

**CanvasLayer order (critical)**
- 127: motion blur
- 128: subtitles / mission text
- 190: street overlay
- 200: transition fade

## Cafe Scene (Dialogue -> Cinema)
- Movement is disabled; mouse look remains active.
- Ambient tracks: cafe ambience, rain muffled, cafe music.
- RainMuffled bus created with low-pass filter for exterior rain.
- Cinema preload starts 1s after load; penulti load request runs in parallel.
- Dialogue audio + timed subtitles; hard cut at 52.2s.
- See [scripts/scenes/cafe_boot.gd](scripts/scenes/cafe_boot.gd) and [scripts/scenes/cafe_dialogue.gd](scripts/scenes/cafe_dialogue.gd).

---

## Cinema Scene (Flicker -> Penulti)
- Screen emissive shader + projector light flicker.
- Ambient: cinema ambience and film audio (CinemaFilm bus with LPF).
- Penulti is instantiated hidden and runs its LR sequence in the background.
- Hard cut at 27s (12s content + 15s LR buffer).
- See [scripts/scenes/cinema_boot.gd](scripts/scenes/cinema_boot.gd) and [scripts/scenes/cinema_screen.gd](scripts/scenes/cinema_screen.gd).

---

## Penulti Scene (Fog -> Beach)
**Boot sequence (LR/GR)**
- LR camera timeline: sweep right, tilt down, simulate interact, sweep right, then GR at 15s.
- Player input suppressed during LR; released after GR and transition complete.
- GR starts fog/lamp fade and audio.
- See [scripts/scenes/penulti_boot.gd](scripts/scenes/penulti_boot.gd).

**Fog + Lamp Fade**
- Fog ramps from t=14.14s to 52s; max density at 12.5.
- Lamp energy fades to zero with 1% noise flicker until 60s.
- CityMegaNode hidden at 60s.

**Beach Reveal + Outro**
- Beach env targets are defined in penulti boot (sky, ambient, fog, glow).
- Beach sun energy is zeroed at start and ramped during reveal.
- Outro duration: 40s; hides BeachMegaNode and CityMegaNode at end.

**Audio**
- Non-looping: penulti_part_1.ogg.
- Crossfade to penulti_loop.ogg 2s before part_1 ends.

**Debug + Flow Controls**
- debug_skip_to_beach and debug_player_position.
- alley_end_scene can hard-cut after the alley sequence; blank means fade to black.

**Required scene nodes (by path)**
- Player, WorldEnvironmentCity, CityMegaNode, BeachMegaNode, BenchParent, SittingBoxPhysics/InteractableIndicator.
